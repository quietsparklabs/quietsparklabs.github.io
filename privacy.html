Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy covers QuietSpark Labs and its internal tool, the QuietSpark Uploader (the "App").

Who uses the App

The App is used only by QuietSpark Labs to manage QuietSpark Labs' own YouTube channels. It is not available to the public, and no outside users sign in to it.

What the App uses YouTube for

The App uses YouTube API Services to upload QuietSpark Labs' own videos and to set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists and publish times. It does not read, collect or store information about viewers or any other YouTube users.

Data we access and store

  • Google account authorization: an OAuth access token for QuietSpark Labs' own Google account, stored only on QuietSpark Labs' own computer inside our automation software. It is used only to upload and manage our own videos.
  • Video information: the titles, descriptions and upload status of our own videos, kept on our own computer to track what has been posted.

We do not sell, share, or transfer any data obtained through YouTube API Services to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

YouTube and Google

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using YouTube you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access granted to the App can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is revoked, the stored token is deleted from our computer.

Data retention and deletion

Stored tokens are kept only while the App is in use and are deleted when access is revoked or the App is retired. Upload records for our own videos are kept for our own bookkeeping and can be deleted at any time.
